What this is

This is a research and education prototype by Aarya Mallik. It estimates building damage from before and after images using a deep learning model trained on the xBD / xView2 dataset. It is intended for non-commercial use. The dataset has its own licence terms.

Do not rely on it for decisions

Results can be wrong, especially for floods, regions and image types that were not in the training data. The model has not been validated on the August 2026 flood in Nepal or in any other specific region. Do not use it for rescue, evacuation, insurance, legal, engineering or official damage assessment decisions. Use field assessments and official sources such as NDRRMA and Copernicus EMS instead.
