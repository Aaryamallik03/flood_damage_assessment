Privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

This site is a student research prototype by Aarya Mallik. This page explains what happens to the images you upload and what the site records.

Images you upload

The model runs inside your web browser. When you press Assess damage, your two images are read and processed on your own device. They are not uploaded to any server, not stored, not used to train or improve the model, and not shared with anyone.

Only use imagery you have the right to use. Do not use images of identifiable people.

What the site records

The web server and the company hosting it may keep standard access logs, which usually include your IP address, the time, and the page or endpoint requested. These logs are used to keep the service working and to investigate abuse.

Cookies, analytics and third parties

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising tools. To run the model, your browser downloads a script called ONNX Runtime Web from the jsDelivr content delivery network (cdn.jsdelivr.net), which can see your IP address when it serves that file. No fonts or images are loaded from other companies. Links to other sites (NDRRMA, UNDP, Copernicus, GitHub) are governed by the policies of those sites.

Your choices

You can use the site without giving any personal information. To ask about data held in server logs, contact aaryamallik43@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
