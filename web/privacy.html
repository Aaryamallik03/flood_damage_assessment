Privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

This site is a student research prototype by Aarya Mallik. This page explains what happens to the images you upload and what the site records.

Images you upload

When you press Assess damage, your two images are sent to the server running this prototype. The server saves them to a temporary folder, runs the model, returns the result, and deletes them straight away. They are not stored afterwards, not used to train or improve the model, and not shared with anyone.

Only upload imagery you have the right to use. Do not upload images of identifiable people.

What the site records

The web server and the company hosting it may keep standard access logs, which usually include your IP address, the time, and the page or endpoint requested. These logs are used to keep the service working and to investigate abuse.

Cookies, analytics and third parties

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising tools. It loads no fonts, scripts or images from other companies. Links to other sites (NDRRMA, UNDP, Copernicus, GitHub) are governed by those sites' own policies.

Your choices

You can use the site without giving any personal information. To ask about data held in server logs, contact aaryamallik43@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
